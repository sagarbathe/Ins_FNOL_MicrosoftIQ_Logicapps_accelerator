# Contoso Auto Insurance — Personal Auto Policy (PAP) Wording & Exclusions

Document ID: KB-POL-001
Effective: 2024-01-01
Applies to: All Personal Auto Policy (PAP) forms issued by Contoso Insurance in the East States and South States regions.

## 1. Coverage Parts

### 1.1 Part A — Liability Coverage
Pays for bodily injury (BI) and property damage (PD) that the insured is legally liable for arising out of the ownership, maintenance, or use of a covered auto.
- Standard limits: $100,000/$300,000 BI, $100,000 PD (higher limits available up to $500,000 CSL).
- Excludes: intentional acts, use of vehicle for livery/ride-share without endorsement, racing, or use by an excluded driver.

### 1.2 Part B — Medical Payments Coverage
Pays reasonable medical expenses for bodily injury caused by an accident, regardless of fault, up to the selected limit ($5,000 / $10,000 / $25,000).

### 1.3 Part C — Uninsured/Underinsured Motorist (UM/UIM)
Pays for bodily injury (and in some states property damage) caused by an uninsured or underinsured driver. Required to be offered in all states of operation; may be rejected in writing by the insured.

### 1.4 Part D — Coverage for Damage to Your Auto (Physical Damage)
- **Collision**: Pays for damage to the covered auto from collision with another object or overturn, subject to deductible ($250/$500/$1,000).
- **Comprehensive (Other Than Collision)**: Pays for loss from causes other than collision — fire, theft, glass breakage, hail, flood, animal contact, vandalism — subject to deductible.
- **Rental Reimbursement**: Optional endorsement, pays up to $30/day, 30-day max, while covered auto is being repaired for a covered loss.

## 2. Standard Exclusions (Applies to All Parts Unless Endorsed)
1. **Racing/Speed Contests** — No coverage for loss occurring while the vehicle is used in a prearranged or organized racing or speed contest.
2. **Commercial/Livery Use** — No coverage for loss while vehicle is used to carry persons or property for a fee (ride-share, delivery) unless the Ride-Share Endorsement (Form CI-RS-100) is attached.
3. **Wear and Tear / Mechanical Breakdown** — Not a covered peril under Comprehensive or Collision.
4. **Intentional Loss** — No coverage if the loss was intentionally caused by or at the direction of an insured.
5. **War / Nuclear Hazard** — Standard war and nuclear exclusion applies.
6. **Undisclosed / Excluded Drivers** — No liability coverage extends to a driver specifically excluded by endorsement (Form CI-XD-050).
7. **Business Use of Non-Rated Vehicle** — Loss is excluded if the vehicle was regularly used for business purposes not disclosed at underwriting, unless rated accordingly.
8. **Salvage/Total Loss Fraud Indicators** — Any claim involving material misrepresentation of vehicle condition, mileage, or prior salvage title voids Part D coverage for that loss (see KB-SIU-003).

## 3. Deductible Rules
- Comprehensive and Collision deductibles apply per occurrence.
- Glass-only comprehensive claims may have a $0 deductible if the "Full Glass" endorsement is present.
- Deductible waived in full-liability-of-other-party claims when subrogation is expected to succeed at 100% (see KB-SUB-005), pending recovery.

## 4. Claim Reporting Requirements
- Policyholder (or authorized reporter) must notify Contoso Insurance within a "reasonable time," defined operationally as within 5 business days of the loss, except when reporting is delayed due to hospitalization or incapacity.
- First Notice of Loss (FNOL) must capture: date/time/location of loss, description of how the loss occurred, police report number (if applicable), other party information, injuries, and photos when available.
- Claims reported more than 30 days after the loss date require a Late Reporting Justification note from the adjuster before payment authorization.

## 5. State Variations Summary (East States / South States)
| Topic | East States | South States |
|---|---|---|
| UM/UIM default | Included unless rejected in writing | Included unless rejected in writing |
| Minimum liability limits | 25/50/25 | 30/60/25 |
| Diminished value claims | First-party DV not payable; third-party DV payable per case law | First-party and third-party DV payable per statute |
| PIP (No-Fault) | Not applicable (tort state) | Not applicable (tort state) |
| Total loss threshold | 75% of ACV (total loss formula) | 70% of ACV (total loss formula) |

See KB-REG-004 for full regulatory detail by state.
