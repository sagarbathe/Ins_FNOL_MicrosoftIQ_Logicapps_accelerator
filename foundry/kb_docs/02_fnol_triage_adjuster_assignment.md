# FNOL Triage & Adjuster Assignment — Operating Guidelines

Document ID: KB-TRI-002
Effective: 2024-01-01
Audience: Auto Claims Intake Team, Claims Adjusters, Claims Supervisors

## 1. Purpose
This document defines how a First Notice of Loss (FNOL) is triaged, severity-scored, and routed to the correct adjuster tier immediately after intake, before formal investigation begins.

## 2. Severity Tiers
| Tier | Description | Routing |
|---|---|---|
| Tier 1 — Minor | No injuries, single vehicle or low-speed multi-vehicle, estimated damage < $5,000, no fraud/subrogation signals | Auto-assign to Express Claims Team (self-service / fast-track) |
| Tier 2 — Standard | No/minor injuries, estimated damage $5,000–$25,000, straightforward liability | Assign to Standard Adjuster pool by region/workload |
| Tier 3 — Complex | Injuries reported, estimated damage > $25,000, disputed liability, commercial vehicle involved, or total-loss likely | Assign to Senior Adjuster with Total-Loss/Injury certification |
| Tier 4 — Special Handling | Any fraud signal present (see KB-SIU-003), litigation threat, catastrophe-related, or VIP/large-account policyholder | Route to SIU liaison + Senior Adjuster jointly; supervisor notification required |

## 3. Adjuster Assignment Rules
1. **Region match**: Adjuster's assigned territory must include the loss location region (East States or South States).
2. **Workload balance**: Among eligible adjusters in the region/tier, assign to the adjuster with the fewest open claims (target ≤ 18 open claims per adjuster).
3. **Certification match**: Total-loss claims require an adjuster with the "Total Loss Certified" credential. Injury claims (BI severity Moderate or higher) require "Bodily Injury Certified" credential.
4. **Continuity rule**: If the policyholder has an existing open claim with an assigned adjuster within the last 12 months, prefer reassigning to the same adjuster if capacity allows.
5. **SLA for first adjuster contact**:
   - Tier 1: within 24 hours (automated acknowledgment can substitute for initial contact)
   - Tier 2: within 24 hours
   - Tier 3: within 4 hours
   - Tier 4: within 2 hours, with supervisor cc'd

## 4. Required FNOL Data Elements for Triage
- Loss date/time, loss location (state/region), weather/road conditions
- Number of vehicles involved, number of parties injured
- Estimated point-of-impact and damage description
- Police report filed (Y/N) and report number
- Vehicle drivability (drivable / not drivable / towed)
- Policy status at time of loss (active, lapsed, pending cancellation)
- Any prior claims within 24 months on this policy or vehicle

## 5. Automatic Escalation Triggers (Escalate Regardless of Estimated Damage)
- Any reported injury described as "serious," "hospitalized," or "fatality"
- Fire or vehicle total burn loss
- Any party is a minor operating the vehicle without a listed-driver endorsement
- Loss location is outside the policy's rated territory (2 states or more away)
- Third-party attorney has already contacted Contoso Insurance
- Vehicle reported stolen or claim involves a mysterious disappearance

## 6. Communication Requirements
- Acknowledgment email/SMS to policyholder within 1 hour of FNOL submission (automated).
- Adjuster introduction (name, phone, claim number) within the SLA windows in Section 3.
- Status update cadence: Tier 1–2 weekly; Tier 3–4 every 3 business days minimum.
- All Teams/email notifications to internal stakeholders (adjuster, supervisor, SIU) must reference the Claim Number and Tier in the subject line, e.g., `[CLM-2024-00193][Tier 3] New FNOL Assigned`.

## 7. Sample Triage Decision Trace (for reference)
> Claim CLM-2024-00193: Rear-end collision, East States, no injuries reported, estimated damage $8,200, no fraud signals, policyholder has one prior claim (closed, no-fault) 8 months ago.
> Decision: Tier 2 — Standard. Assign to Standard Adjuster pool, East States region, workload-balanced. SLA: 24-hour first contact.
