# SIU Fraud Referral Playbook — Auto Claims

Document ID: KB-SIU-003
Effective: 2024-01-01
Audience: Claims Adjusters, Special Investigations Unit (SIU)

## 1. Purpose
Defines the red-flag indicators, scoring approach, and referral process used to identify auto claims requiring Special Investigations Unit (SIU) review during or shortly after FNOL intake.

## 2. Red Flag Indicator Categories

### 2.1 Policy-Related Indicators
- Policy inception or coverage increase within 30 days of the loss date ("early loss")
- Policy history of frequent lapses and reinstatements
- Loss occurs immediately before a scheduled non-renewal or cancellation

### 2.2 Claim Circumstance Indicators
- Loss reported significantly later than it occurred (reporting delay > 7 days with no injury/incapacity justification)
- Vague, inconsistent, or evolving description of how the loss occurred across statements
- No police report despite circumstances (multi-vehicle collision, hit-and-run) that would typically warrant one
- Claimed loss location has no corroborating witnesses, cameras, or third parties
- Single-vehicle loss with disproportionately high claimed damage relative to described impact

### 2.3 Party & Vehicle Indicators
- Claimant, repair shop, and/or towing company have appeared together on multiple prior claims (referral network pattern)
- Vehicle has a prior salvage or flood title not disclosed at underwriting
- Vehicle mileage at loss is inconsistent with prior inspection/appraisal records
- Insured requests payment be made directly to themselves rather than a repair facility, for a vehicle claimed as "totaled"

### 2.4 Financial Indicators
- Insured has a recent history of financial distress (bankruptcy filing, foreclosure) disclosed elsewhere in file
- Claimed total loss value is disproportionately higher than comparable market vehicles
- Multiple claims filed by same insured across different lines (auto, property) in a short period

## 3. Fraud Scoring Model (Reference Only — actual scoring computed by FraudSignal data table)
Each indicator present contributes weighted points; claims scoring above the threshold are auto-flagged:
- 0–24 points: No SIU referral required
- 25–49 points: Adjuster discretion; document rationale if not referred
- 50+ points: Mandatory SIU referral before any payment is authorized

## 4. Referral Process
1. Adjuster (or automated triage system) identifies one or more red flags during FNOL intake or investigation.
2. Adjuster completes the SIU Referral Form, citing specific indicators observed (do not rely on "gut feel" alone — cite Section 2 categories).
3. Referral is submitted to the SIU intake queue; SIU acknowledges within 2 business days.
4. **Claim payment is placed on hold** pending SIU review for any claim with a Mandatory referral (Section 3, 50+ points).
5. SIU completes initial review within 10 business days and returns one of: Cleared (no fraud indicators substantiated), Continue Investigation (extended review, up to 60 days), or Confirmed Fraud (claim denied, refer to Legal/SIU for further action, potential law enforcement referral).
6. All SIU determinations are logged against the Claim record with the reviewing investigator's name and date.

## 5. Adjuster Responsibilities While SIU Review Is Pending
- Do not disclose to the claimant that a fraud investigation is underway.
- Continue routine claim administration (acknowledgments, non-financial correspondence) but do not issue payment or close the claim.
- Cooperate with SIU requests for additional documentation, EUO (Examination Under Oath) scheduling, or recorded statements.

## 6. Confirmed Fraud Outcomes
- Claim is denied under the Concealment or Fraud condition of the policy.
- Case is referred to Contoso Insurance Legal and, where required by state law, to the state Department of Insurance Fraud Bureau.
- Policy may be cancelled/rescinded per underwriting guidelines and applicable state law.
