# State Regulatory Requirements — East States / South States (Auto Claims)

Document ID: KB-REG-004
Effective: 2024-01-01
Audience: Claims Adjusters, Compliance

## 1. Purpose
Summarizes the claims-handling regulatory requirements that differ between the two regions in which Contoso Insurance's Auto book is written: **East States** and **South States** (synthetic/composite regions used for this accelerator, modeled on common unfair claims practices act provisions).

## 2. Prompt Claim Handling Timelines
| Requirement | East States | South States |
|---|---|---|
| Acknowledge receipt of claim | Within 10 calendar days | Within 15 calendar days |
| Begin investigation | Within 15 calendar days of notice | Within 20 calendar days of notice |
| Accept or deny claim (after proof of loss) | Within 30 calendar days | Within 45 calendar days |
| Pay claim after acceptance | Within 10 business days | Within 15 business days |
| Written explanation required if claim denied | Yes, citing specific policy provision | Yes, citing specific policy provision |

## 3. Total Loss Valuation Requirements
- **East States**: Total loss threshold is 75% of Actual Cash Value (ACV). Insurer must provide the claimant a detailed valuation report including at least two comparable vehicles or a recognized third-party valuation service report, and disclose any deductions itemized (mileage, condition, prior damage).
- **South States**: Total loss threshold is 70% of ACV. Insurer must offer the option of replacement vehicle sourcing OR cash settlement, and must include sales tax and title/license fees in the settlement calculation per statute.
- Both regions: Salvage value retained by insurer must be disclosed and, if the insured requests to retain the vehicle, salvage value is deducted from the settlement.

## 4. Diminished Value (DV) Claims
- **East States**: First-party diminished value is not payable under Part D. Third-party diminished value claims are payable based on case law precedent when the insured's vehicle is repaired after a not-at-fault loss.
- **South States**: Both first-party and third-party diminished value claims are payable per statute, using a documented DV calculation methodology (17c formula or equivalent).

## 5. Rental / Loss of Use
- Both regions require "reasonable" rental reimbursement during repair for at-fault claims where Rental Reimbursement coverage is present, or for not-at-fault claims payable by the at-fault carrier regardless of the insured's own coverage selections.

## 6. Recorded Statements & Examinations Under Oath (EUO)
- Insured must cooperate with reasonable requests for recorded statements and EUOs as a condition of coverage in both regions.
- **South States** requires that the insured be given at least 5 business days' notice before an EUO and be permitted to have counsel present.

## 7. Unfair Claims Settlement Practices — Prohibited Conduct (Both Regions)
- Misrepresenting policy provisions relevant to the claim.
- Failing to adopt reasonable standards for prompt investigation.
- Compelling claimants to litigate by offering substantially less than amounts ultimately recovered.
- Failing to affirm or deny coverage within a reasonable time after proof of loss is completed.

## 8. Subrogation Notice Requirements
- Insurer must notify the insured before pursuing subrogation against a third party if the insured's deductible is included in the subrogation demand, and must share recovered deductible amounts proportionally (pro-rata) with the insured. See KB-SUB-005 for subrogation identification methodology.
